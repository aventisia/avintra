# SignIn to Aventisia

Sign into Aventisia either through your email address.

## Create account

Enter your details to get started:

- **First Name**
- **Last Name**
- **Email**
- **Password**
- Agree to the [Terms & Conditions](https://aventisia.com/terms-of-service)

A six digit code will be sent to your email address - this code is valid for 5 minutes
Enter the code on the Sign-up screen to sign in to your account

## Log In with your email address

To sign in to Aventisia with your email address:

- Head to [login page](https://avintra.aventisia.com/accounts/login)
- Enter your email address and password of your choice
- Click Login

## Want to reset your password?

No worries, we'll send you reset instructions.

- Enter **Email:** address and click `Continue`
- Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email.
- Enter New password and Re-Login

You'll stay signed in unless you manually log out, or if cookies are deleted when clearing your browsing data.

## Signing out of Aventisia

To sign out of Aventisia, click your name in the top-left, then click Sign out at the bottom of the pop-up screen.
