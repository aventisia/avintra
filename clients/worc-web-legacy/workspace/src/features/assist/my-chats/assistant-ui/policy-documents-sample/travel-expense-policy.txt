1.	Objective and Scope
The purpose of this policy is to define the guidelines and procedures for business-related travel and expense reimbursements. The policy ensures cost-effectiveness, transparency, and compliance with company financial guidelines.
Scope
•	Applies to all full-time, part-time, and contractual employees undertaking business travel.
•	Covers domestic travel within India and international travel to all countries.
•	Applies to expenses incurred for client meetings, conferences, training, and other business-approved travel.

Key Principles
•	All travel must have a clear, documented business purpose.
•	Employees should seek cost-effective travel arrangements while ensuring safety and productivity.
•	Expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business.
•	Employees must submit all expense claims with appropriate documentation.

4.3 Hotel Rules
•	Rates shown are per-night limits. Exceeding limits requires prior written approval from the HOD and Finance.
•	Hotel must be booked via the approved corporate travel portal or through the Admin/Travel desk. Direct bookings require explicit justification.
•	Only single-occupancy rooms are covered. Double rooms for personal companions are not reimbursable.
•	Mini-bar, laundry (personal), and in-room movies are not reimbursable. Business laundry for trips exceeding 5 nights is reimbursable up to ₹500/day (domestic) or USD 15/day (international).
•	Early check-in or late check-out charges are reimbursable only if necessitated by flight schedules (attach boarding pass proof).
•	Hotel loyalty reward points accrue to the employee personally.
•	For Category X (event cities), the company may negotiate block bookings; employees must use the designated hotel unless unavailable.

5.3 Per Diem Rules
•	Per diem is a flat daily allowance. No receipts are required for per diem reimbursement.
•	If the company or client provides meals (e.g., at a conference), the meal component is deducted: breakfast = 20%, lunch = 30%, dinner = 50% of the Meals/Day amount.
•	For partial travel days: departure before 12 noon or arrival after 8 PM = full day per diem. All other partial days = 50% per diem.
•	International per diem rates are in USD and will be reimbursed in INR at the RBI reference rate on the date of the transaction or expense submission (whichever is lower).

•	All flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance for domestic and 21 days for international to avail the best fares.

6.3 Ground Transportation
•	Airport/station transfers: Ola/Uber preferred. Reimbursable at actuals with app receipt.
•	Rental cars: require prior approval; Band D+ only for self-drive. Shared cabs are preferred.
•	Personal vehicle use: reimbursed at ₹12/km (two-wheeler) or ₹18/km (four-wheeler) with fuel receipts.
•	Tolls, parking at client site: reimbursable at actuals.
•	Auto-rickshaw/metro/bus: reimbursable at actuals without receipts (up to ₹200/day).

 
7. International Travel — Special Rules
7.1 Pre-Travel Requirements
1.	Travel Authorization Form (TAF) submitted in RazorpayX Expense minimum 10 business days before travel.
2.	Business justification, trip agenda, and cost estimate to be attached.
3.	Visa, travel insurance (mandatory), and foreign exchange arrangement must be completed before departure.
4.	For travel to countries with travel advisories (MEA Level 3+), additional approval from CHRO/CEO is required.

7.2 Foreign Exchange & Cards
•	Company-issued corporate credit card to be used wherever possible.
•	Forex cash advances: up to USD 500 per trip (Band A,B and C), USD 1,000 (Band D and E). Unspent forex must be surrendered within 5 days of return.
•	Forex card / multi-currency card is preferred over cash for all international expenses.
•	Reimbursement of personal foreign currency expenditure: at RBI reference rate on the date of submission or transaction (whichever is lower).

7.3 Travel Insurance
•	Mandatory travel insurance is arranged by the company for all international trips.
•	Coverage includes: medical (USD 500,000), trip cancellation, baggage loss, and personal accident.
•	Employees must carry the insurance card / policy number during travel.
•	Medical emergencies: call the insurer helpline first. Out-of-pocket medical expenses are reimbursable up to the policy limit.

7.4 Visa & Immigration
•	Visa fees, FRRO registration, and embassy charges are fully reimbursable.
•	Passport renewal for official travel purposes: reimbursable with HOD approval.
•	Travel vaccination fees (if required for destination country): reimbursable.

8.3 Advance / Imprest
•	Travel advances can be requested in RazorpayX Expense under 'Cash Advance Request'.
•	Advance must be settled within 15 days of return by submitting the expense report.
•	Unreconciled advances after 30 days will be recovered from salary.

8.4 GST Compliance
•	Employees must capture the GST number of vendors (hotels, airlines) in RazorpayX for input tax credit (ITC) eligibility.
•	Hotels and airlines must issue GST-compliant invoices; employees should specifically request these.
•	Expenses without GST invoices where applicable may lead to partial reimbursement only.

8.5 Currency & FX
•	Submit all international expenses in the original transacted currency. RazorpayX will auto-convert at RBI reference rate.
•	Attach bank/card statement showing the actual INR debit for reconciliation.

10. Best Practices & Compliance
10.1 Before Travel
•	Always book travel via the authorized portal — last-minute bookings cost 30–50% more and will attract scrutiny.
•	Obtain all required pre-approvals before making any bookings.
•	Download the RazorpayX Expense mobile app before travel to capture receipts on the go.
•	Check if a client/conference provides accommodation — avoid double-booking.
•	Review the company's Corporate Travel Safety Guidelines for the destination country.

10.2 During Travel
•	Capture receipts immediately after each transaction using the RazorpayX app photo feature.
•	For hotel stays, always request a GST-compliant tax invoice at checkout.
•	Avoid cash transactions wherever possible — use corporate or personal card for traceability.
•	Do not share hotel/transport details on public social media (security risk).
•	Contact the travel helpdesk immediately for any emergency (medical, security, lost documents).

10.3 After Travel
•	Submit expense report within 15 business days. Late submissions (> 30 days) will require HOD + Finance exception approval.
•	Reconcile all cash advances before submitting.
•	Return unused foreign currency within 5 business days.
•	Provide a trip report for international travel > 5 days to your HOD and HR.

10.4 Policy Compliance & Audit
•	Finance conducts quarterly random audits of 10% of all expense reports.
•	Any falsification or inflation of expenses constitutes gross misconduct and may lead to disciplinary action including termination.
•	Duplicate claims (same expense claimed more than once) are detected automatically by RazorpayX and flagged for investigation.
•	Employees who identify policy violations by others should report them to hr@company.com (confidential whistleblower channel).
•	Policy exceptions approved more than 3 times in a year for the same employee will trigger an HR review.

10.5 Sustainability & Green Travel
•	Prefer train over flight for journeys under 4 hours (lower carbon footprint).
•	Share rides to/from airports where colleagues travel on the same route.
•	Choose hotels with green/sustainability certifications where available within policy limits.
•	Opt for video/virtual meetings as the first option before approving travel.

 
11. Policy Administration
11.1 Policy Owner
This policy is jointly owned by the Finance (CFO's office) and HR teams. Any updates require co-sign from both CFO and CHRO.

11.2 Review Cycle
•	Policy is reviewed annually, typically in Q1 of each financial year.
•	Ad-hoc revisions may be made for significant macroeconomic changes (e.g., >15% INR/USD movement, major inflation events).
•	All revisions are communicated company-wide via email and updated in RazorpayX automatically.

11.3 Exceptions
•	All exceptions must be documented and approved before the expense is incurred wherever possible.
•	Exception requests submitted via RazorpayX Expense under the 'Exception Approval' workflow.
•	Retroactive exceptions (post-travel) are granted only in genuine emergencies.
