Objective
The objective of this policy is to enable Aventisia employees to maintain a healthy work life balance. It defines various types of leaves that can be availed by the employee for sickness, emergency, personal work, rest or recreation. 
Scope
This policy is applicable to all employees of Aventisia Technologies Pvt. Ltd. and its subsidiaries, group companies and/or affiliates operating in India.
General Guidelines
•	Family is defined as parents, spouse, brothers, sisters, children, parents-in-law, and grandparents. 
•	Entitlement of leaves (as given in the tables) assumes that the employee has worked at least 15 days in the relevant month. Anyone who joins on or before 15th of a month shall be entitled to accrue leave for that month. Post 15th of the month, joiners shall accrue leave next month onwards. 
•	When on Leave Without Pay (LWP), the sandwiched weekly offs are also treated as LWP. 
•	Sick leave cannot be availed for any purposes other than the employee’s medical problems / complaints. 
•	Casual leaves must be availed within the calendar year. Any casual leave balance or any unutilized casual leave as on 31 December shall lapse and cannot be carried forward to the next year. 
•	An employee must plan all leaves in advance (except for sick leaves) and take formal approval on email from the supervisor when availing any leave. 
Maternity Leave (for details, please refer to Maternity Benefits Act, 1961 or applicable Shops and Commercial Establishment Act)
•	As per the provisions of the Act, no women employee shall be entitled to maternity benefit unless she has actually worked in Aventisia for a period of not less than eighty days in the twelve months immediately preceding the date of her expected delivery
•	Maternity leave shall be paid in full wages. Maternity benefits shall be available as per the Maternity Benefits Act, 1961 (as amended) or applicable Shops and Commercial Establishments Act, as per applicability. 
•	In maternity cases, if an employee wants to work beyond seven months of pregnancy, as the case may be, they shall be required to submit a ‘Fit to work’ certificate from a registered medical practitioner. 
•	Notwithstanding anything contained here in this policy, if a woman employee has only one surviving child, the maximum period of maternity benefit shall be twenty-six weeks of which not more than eight weeks may precede the date of expected delivery. The maximum period of maternity benefit applicable to a woman employee having two or more than two surviving children shall be twelve weeks of which not more than six weeks shall precede the date of expected delivery. 
•	Commissioning mother shall be entitled to twelve weeks of maternity leaves from the date the child is handed over to the commissioning mother. 

•	A woman employee who delivers a child and returns to duty after delivery shall be allowed, in addition to interval for rest, two nursing breaks of thirty minutes duration each, until the child is fifteen months old. No wages shall be deducted for nursing breaks or for lighter work being assigned to a woman employee by reason of pregnancy. 
•	In case a women employee absents herself from work as per the provisions of the Act or SCE Act; she shall not be discharged / dismissed / removed from service during the period of; or on account of such absence from duty, or notice of discharge or dismissal or removal shall not be given to her on such a day that the notice will expire during such absence, and /or any conditions of her service shall not be altered to her disadvantage. 
•	In case a woman employee dies before receiving maternity benefit, the same shall be paid to her nominee. If there is not such nominee, it shall be paid to her legal representative or legal heir or to the person who undertakes the care of the child surviving the concerned woman employee. Provide the maternity benefit shall be payable only for the days up to and including the day of her death. Provided further that where a woman having been delivered for a child, dies during her delivery or during the period immediately following the date of her delivery for which she is entitled for the maternity benefit, leaving behind in either case the child, the maternity benefit for that entire period will be paid but it the child also dies during the given period, then, for the days up to and including the date of the death of the child. 
•	A woman employee shall not be required or allowed to work and no woman employee shall work in the / during the period of eighteen weeks or six weeks, as the case may be, immediately following the date of her delivery and/or for six weeks immediately following the date of her miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy. 
Optional Holidays
•	In NOIDA, Pune and Jaipur, optional holidays (OH) can be adjusted against any other working day by giving a compensatory off
•	In Gurugram, there is no concept of adjusting OH against any other working day by giving a compensatory off. OH, can only be adjusted against any other OH from the published list. 
Compensatory Leave
Compensatory Leave is given in lieu of working on Optional Holidays / Weekly Offs. All such work has to be sanctioned by the supervisor in advance and must not violate maximum working hours per week applicable as per relevant state laws. 
This leave shall be given to employees (provided they work for more than six hours on an OH or weekly off) who are not covered under extra time / any other monetary incentive scheme.
Adoption Leave
•	Adoption leave shall be applicable for any employee (whether married or single) who adopts a child. 
•	In order to obtain Adoption Leave, the employee has to submit valid evidence of the child being placed with them in foster care or final adoption decree from the jurisdictional court. 
•	Adoption Leave can begin from the date the child is placed under foster care of the adopting parents, subject to the said employee submitting the written confirmation of the placement provided by the Specialized Adoption Agency to the adopting parents.
•	The duration of adoption leave would be as per the age of the adopted child (at the time of adoption):
o	Up to 1 year: 12 weeks
o	1 to 3 years: 6 weeks
o	3 to 5 years: 3 weeks
