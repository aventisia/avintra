The **Queues** page shows all the Queues you have access to and the work items in those queues. Process each item, view its details and timeline, and complete or escalate it as needed.

## Purpose

This page is where you handle the work that has been routed to you. Each item represents a task that needs your attention.

## The table

The page shows a list of work items with columns such as:

- **Item** — the work item identifier.
- **Queue** — which queue the item came from.
- **Status** — the item's current state.
- **Assigned To** — who is handling the item.

## How to use it

1. Open a work item to see its details and timeline.
2. Process the item (e.g. review, respond, or complete it).
3. Escalate or reassign items that need someone else's attention.

## Tips

- Work through items in priority order.
- Use the timeline to understand the item's history.
