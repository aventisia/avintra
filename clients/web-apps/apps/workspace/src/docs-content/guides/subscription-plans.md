
The **Subscription Plans** page lets you define the plans available to tenants. Manage the pricing and features of each plan.

## Purpose

Use this page to create and manage the subscription plans that tenants can choose from.

## The table

The page shows a table of plans with columns such as:

- **Name** — the plan's name.
- **Currency** — the plan's billing currency.
- **Price** — the plan's price.
- **Billing Frequency** — how often the plan is billed.
- **Max Credits** — the credits included in the plan.
- **Max Published Items** — the published items limit.
- **History Retention Days** — how long history is kept.
- **Max Spaces / Storage Limit / Max Users** — resource limits.
- **Created By / Created On** — who created the plan and when.

## Row actions (the three dots)

Each row has a **⋮** menu with actions such as:

- **View** — see the plan's details.
- **Edit** — change the plan's details.
- **Delete** — remove the plan.

## Creating a plan

Click the **Create Plan** button. Fill in:

- **Name** — the plan's name.
- **Price** — the plan's price.
- **Features** — what the plan includes.

## Tips

- Keep plan names and pricing clear and consistent.
- Review plans periodically to ensure they match your offerings.
