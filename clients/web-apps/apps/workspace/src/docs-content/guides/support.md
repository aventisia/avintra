The **Support** page lets you raise and track support tickets. Get help from the Avintra team when you need it.

## Purpose

Use this page to report issues, ask questions, and follow the progress of your service requests and change requests.

## The table

The page shows a table of support tickets with columns such as:

- **Ticket** — the ticket's title, number, who opened it, and when.
- **Type** — whether it is a Service Request or a Change Request.
- **Status** — the ticket's current state (e.g. New, In Progress, Closed).
- **Created By** — who raised the ticket.
- **Created On** — when the ticket was raised.
- **Total Comments** — how many comments the ticket has.
- **Total Attachments** — how many attachments the ticket has.

Use the search box to find a ticket by title or ticket number. Select one or more rows to delete tickets in bulk.

## Row actions (the three dots)

Each row has a **⋮** menu with actions such as:

- **Open** — open the ticket to see its details, comments, and attachments.
- **Edit** — update the ticket.
- **Delete** — remove the ticket.

## Creating a ticket

Click the **Create Support Ticket** button. Fill in:

- **Request Type** — Service Request or Change Request. Change Request appears only when your tenant has it enabled.
- **Title** — a short summary of the request.
- **Description** — the full details of the request.
- **Required By** — the date the service request is needed by (Service Request only).

For a Change Request, also fill in:

- **Agent** — the agent the change applies to.
- **Change Summary** — what should change.
- **Change Reason** — why the change is needed.
- **Expected Behavior** — how the agent should behave after the change.
- **Requested Timeline** — when the change is needed.

## Ticket details

Open a ticket to see its metadata, description, and change-request details. You can:

- **Mark as In Progress** or **Close Ticket**.
- Add, download, and delete attachments.
- Read comments and add a new comment.

Closed tickets cannot receive new comments or attachments.

## Tips

- Provide as much detail as possible to speed up resolution.
- Use the search box to find open tickets by title or ticket number.
