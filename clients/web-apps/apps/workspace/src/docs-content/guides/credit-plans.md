# Credit Plans

The **Credit Plans** page (under Super Admin) lets you manage credit plans and pricing. Define how credits are sold and consumed.

## Purpose

Use this page to create and manage the credit plans available to tenants. Credits are the unit of consumption for agent usage.

## The table

The page shows a table of credit plans with columns such as:

- **Name** — the plan's name.
- **Credits** — how many credits the plan provides.
- **Price** — the plan's price.
- **Status** — whether the plan is active.

## Row actions (the three dots)

Each row has a **⋮** menu with actions such as:

- **Edit** — change the plan's details.
- **Delete** — remove the plan.

## Creating a plan

Click the **Create Plan** button. Fill in:

- **Name** — the plan's name.
- **Credits** — how many credits the plan provides.
- **Price** — the plan's price.

## Tips

- Align credit plans with your pricing strategy.
- Review plans periodically to ensure they match your offerings.
