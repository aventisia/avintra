The **Tenant** page shows your tenant's profile and settings. Use it to manage your organization's account details.

## Purpose

This page is the overview of your tenant. From here you can view and manage your tenant's profile, billing, and plan information.

## What you can do

The page is organised into tabs:

- **Overview** — your tenant's name and key details.
- **Payment Methods** — manage the cards used to pay for your plan.
- **Billing History** — review past invoices and payments.
- **Billing Details** — your company and billing address.
- **Preferences** — tenant-level preferences.
- **Notifications** — configure notification settings.

## Tips

- Keep your tenant profile up to date.
- Review your plan and billing regularly to avoid surprises.
