
The **Tenant** page (under Tenant Admin) shows your tenant's profile and settings. Use it to manage your organization's account details.

## Purpose

This page is the overview of your tenant. From here you can view and manage your tenant's profile, billing, and plan information.

## What you can do

- View your tenant's name and details.
- Manage your subscription plan and billing.
- Review usage and credits.

## Tips

- Keep your tenant profile up to date.
- Review your plan and billing regularly to avoid surprises.
