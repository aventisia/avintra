
The **Usage** page (under Tenant Admin) shows your tenant's consumption and credits. Monitor how much of your plan you are using.

## Purpose

Use this page to track your resource consumption and credit balance. Understand your usage patterns so you can plan capacity and budget.

## What you can see

- **Credits** — your current credit balance.
- **Usage charts** — how your usage has changed over time.
- **Consumption breakdown** — which agents or features consume the most.

## Tips

- Review usage regularly to avoid running out of credits.
- Use the charts to spot trends and plan ahead.
