# Code of Conduct

Avintra adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies in every project space.

**In short:** be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and give feedback on ideas, not people. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **[contact@aventisia.com](mailto:contact@aventisia.com)**. Reports are handled confidentially by the Avintra maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
